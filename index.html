Our Purpose

The Lake District deserves better than it gets online.

The Lake District is England’s largest national park, home to the country’s highest mountain and its deepest lake, and it had 17.45 million visitors in 2025. Yet much of what is online about it is generic or out of date.

The Lakes Network is three guides, each for a different reader: people planning a visit, walkers working through the Wainwrights, and people who come for the wildlife. We check practical details against official sources when we write a page and when we revise it.

Our income comes from businesses that pay for featured listings and advertising. Paying for a space does not change what we write.
