Our Purpose
The Sefton Coast deserves better than it gets online.
Royal Birkdale has hosted The Open since 1954, more often in that time than any venue except St Andrews, most recently in July 2026. The pinewoods at Formby are part of the most southerly red squirrel stronghold on mainland England. Yet much of what is online about the coast is generic or out of date.
The Sefton Coast Network is four guides, each for a different reader: visitors to Southport, day trips to Formby, golfers, and people who come for the wildlife. We check practical details against official sources when we write a page and when we revise it.
We earn money from advertising, featured places and sponsored articles. Every one of them is labelled on the page, and paying for a space does not change what we write.
